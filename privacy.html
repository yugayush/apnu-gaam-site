Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Apnu Gaam (also called Jain Community Connect) is a private, members-only app for our Jain community. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your phone number or email address, or your Google account's name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile details you provide — name, father's and mother's name, family surname, date of birth, gender, native village, current city, occupation, business name, blood group and willingness to donate blood, a short bio and a profile photo. Only your name, date of birth and city are required.
  • Family information — the family you belong to and relatives on its roster (names, relationships, and optionally dates of birth, anniversaries and photos), added by you, your family or community admins.
  • Content you add — business listings, photos, and requests such as joining a family.
  • Device information — a notification token, if you allow notifications, so the app can alert you about community notices and events.

How we use it

  • To let approved members find and reconnect with family and community members.
  • To show family trees, the business directory, notices and events.
  • To let community admins verify new members and keep the community safe.
  • To send notifications you have agreed to.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the community except the service providers listed below.

Who can see your information

  • Nothing is visible to the public or to people who have not been approved.
  • Approved members can see names, families, relationships, villages, cities and business listings.
  • Your phone number, email and date of birth are visible only according to your privacy settings. Relatives' birthdays and anniversaries are visible only to their own family and admins.
  • Community admins can see the details needed to review and approve accounts and requests.

Service providers

The app's data and photos are stored with Supabase, our database and hosting provider. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy. Notifications are delivered through Expo and Google/Apple push services.

Security

Access to data is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app. Photos are stored privately and shown only to approved members, and location data embedded in uploaded photos is removed.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account and personal information by writing to the contact below. We will act on requests within 30 days.

Children

Only people aged 18 or over can create an account. Younger relatives may appear on a family roster added by their family, with limited details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and tell members in the app about significant changes.
