Apnu Gaam

Ek gaam, ek parivaar — a private app that keeps our Jain community connected.

What it does

  • Families — find your family, see your family tree, and reconnect with relatives.
  • Businesses — discover and support businesses run by our own members.
  • Notices & events — announcements and gatherings from the community.

Members only

Apnu Gaam is not a public social network. Every new account is reviewed and approved by a community admin before it can see anything, and personal details such as phone numbers and birthdays stay private.
